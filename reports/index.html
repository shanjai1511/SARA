How This Report is Made

Methodology

1

Discovery Crawl

SARA's automated crawlers discover product and article URLs across all monitored sites using category-aware pagination and bot-evasion techniques.

2

Full-Page Retrieval

Each URL is fetched and stored. Commerce pages capture product name, brand, list price, selling price, and category. Media pages capture headline, publication date, and trend keywords.

3

Structured Parsing

A per-site parser extracts structured fields using XPath, CSS selectors, JSON-LD schema.org blocks, and site-specific APIs. Data lands in Elasticsearch.

4

Gap Scoring

Trend signals (article velocity, keyword frequency) are cross-referenced with SKU density by category, platform, and price band. Gaps are scored and ranked.
